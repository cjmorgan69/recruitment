How to join the Tumby Bay team

Volunteering becomes part of your lifestyle. Rosters and training are flexible and designed around you, so you can progress at a pace you're comfortable with.

1

Apply online

Submit your application or give the Volunteer Support Unit a call. No medical experience needed.

2

Local chat

A Tumby Bay team leader will call you to talk about availability and what to expect.

3

Train with us

Complete accredited, fully-funded training — mostly on evenings and weekends.

4

Join the roster

Attend emergency and non-emergency calls and become a trusted local responder.

Your training is free

No experience? No problem. Once you sign up, SA Ambulance Service (a Registered Training Organisation, RTO 0264) provides everything you need to become a volunteer ambulance assistant, responder or officer.

Certificate II

HLT21020 Certificate II in Medical Service First Response — your first step into emergency care.

Certificate IV

HLT41120 Certificate IV in Health Care — develop advanced patient assessment and care skills.

Ongoing support

Regular workshops, theory and hands-on practice so you always feel confident on shift.

Frequently asked questions

Becoming a volunteer ambulance officer is rewarding for you and your community. Here are the questions we hear most often.

How do I apply to become a volunteer?

Click the "Apply Now" button to start your application online, or call the Volunteer Support Unit on 1300 175 584 or email SAASVolunteer@sa.gov.au. They'll be happy to have a chat about the Tumby Bay team.

Do I have to pay for my ambulance training?

No. Once you sign up, we provide accredited training free of charge — leading to a Certificate II in Medical Service First Response or a Certificate IV in Health Care. SA Ambulance Service is a Registered Training Organisation (RTO 0264).

I don't have any medical experience. Can I still apply?

Absolutely. You don't need any medical experience or training to become a volunteer recruit. We provide all the training and plenty of support so you feel confident with your skills.

What age do I need to be?

To join as an operational volunteer you must be over 17. There's no maximum age — as long as you're fit, healthy and able to meet our medical requirements.

Can I volunteer if I work full time?

Yes, if you have the time. Many of our volunteers work full time in another field. Operational training is generally held on weekends or evenings. You might also talk to your employer about supporting you to respond to calls from work.
